### Accepted F16 distinction

F16 covers the mechanical contraction after `à` or `de` + definite article is already intended:

```text
à + le   → au
à + les  → aux
de + le  → du
de + les → des
```

Non-contracted comparison forms:

```text
à la / à l’ / de la / de l’
```

F16 must not decide lexical preposition choice and must not confuse identical-looking partitives/quantities with contraction ownership.

### Real source map

Strong anchors:

```text
lesson 3   Je viens du Vietnam.
lesson 13  J'ai mal à la tête.
lesson 24  Je rentre à la maison.
lesson 38  Je suis allée au restaurant.
lesson 38  Je suis rentrée à la maison.
lesson 45  J'ai mal à la tête.
lesson 45  J'ai mal au ventre.
```

Anti-confusion anchors:

```text
lesson 22  Du pain. / Du lait. / Des œufs.
           → partitives, NOT F16 contraction proof

lesson 42  Une bouteille d'eau. / Un paquet de riz. / Un peu de…
           → quantity syntax, NOT F16 ownership
```

Coverage verdict:

| Form | Accepted status |
|---|---|
| `au` | strong learner-facing evidence |
| `à la` | strong learner-facing evidence |
| `du` | real but distributed; surface overlap with partitive `du` |
| contraction-`des` | no equally clean learner-facing source pair |
| `aux` | no clean learner-facing item |
| `à l’ / de la / de l’` | surface forms exist, not a complete safe F16 source system |

Therefore F16 was correctly classified `partial-distributed`: enough to justify coherent teaching, not enough for productive Transfer.
