- Listening is already one of Build39's reliable durable action families;

- the capability increase can therefore be information-retention/task-shape first, without new theme lessons or semantic AI grading.

## Candidate R1 contract boundary

A future proof may define an additive optional `bridgeQuestions` list alongside the current single-question dialogue fields. It must not rewrite current fields or change current learner-facing behavior in the pure proof.

Bounded design rules:

```text

non-empty evidence item IDs

explicit facts only; no subtle semantic inference
no free-text semantic grading
```

Recommended future pure owner:

```text
src/pedagogy/a2-reception-bridge-core.js
```

The pure owner would validate/normalize a detached frozen activity plan only. It must have no DOM, storage, network, learner route, Memory/Error/Evidence write or curriculum mutation.

Question outcomes may be stated only as deterministic `success` / `miss`. Sequence summaries may say `completed` or `clean sequence`; none of these imply A2 mastery, readiness, CEFR attainment, listening mastery or unseen transfer.

A wrong choice remains a miss even if correction is later shown. A later learner-facing integration, if separately authorized, should delay the full transcript until the multi-question sequence is complete so question 1 cannot reveal answers to questions 2–3.
